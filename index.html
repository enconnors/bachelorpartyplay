The play

Five childhood friends reunite for a bachelor party in the woods. But when the groom, Sam, doesn’t show, their celebration turns into a reckoning: with each other, with themselves, and with the effigy they’ve created in Sam’s absence. Bachelor Party! is a dark comedy about myth-making, masculinity, and the search for meaning in the wilderness.
