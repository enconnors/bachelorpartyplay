The play

Five childhood friends reunite for a bachelor party in the woods. But when the groom, Sam, doesn’t show, their celebration turns into a reckoning: with each other, with themselves, and with the effigy they’ve created in Sam’s absence.

Bachelor Party! is a dark comedy about myth-making, masculinity, and the search for meaning in the wilderness.

About the team +

Elliot Connors is a Canadian-American writer based in Brooklyn. Most recently, he was named a Just for Laughs New Face for his animated series OMNI, as well as a finalist for the Humanitas Carol Mendelsohn Award for his feature drama set in Québec. As a playwright, he is a member of the award-winning NYC theater company Colt Coeur and has presented his work at the Public Theater. MFA: NYU. BA: Yale.
